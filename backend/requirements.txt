# Windows-only: patches certifi/ssl to trust the OS certificate store, not
# just certifi's bundled public CA list. Without this, outbound HTTPS calls
# (Google Places, Groq, HuggingFace, Telegram, ...) fail with
# SSL: CERTIFICATE_VERIFY_FAILED on machines where a corporate proxy or
# antivirus does TLS inspection with a root cert that's in the Windows
# trust store but not in certifi's bundle.
